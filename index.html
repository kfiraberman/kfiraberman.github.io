Kfir Aberman

Research Scientist at Google DeepMind

I'm a researcher working at the intersection of generative AI, computer vision, and computer graphics, with a focus on video generation, world models, and intuitive control of generative systems. I’m interested in turning powerful AI models into tools that help people create, explore, and interact with visual worlds.

Previously, I was a founding member of Decart, helping establish its US presence and working on real-time generative video and interactive world models. Before that, I led Personalized Generative AI research at Snap Research and worked as a Research Scientist at Google Research.

Beyond research, I'm an angel investor interested in ambitious technology startups and a member of the American Society for AI. I'm interested in the responsible development of AI and its potential to benefit people.

I earned my Ph.D. at Tel Aviv University, advised by Daniel Cohen-Or. I grew up in Zefat, Israel, spent three wonderful years as a researcher in China, and now live in the San Francisco Bay Area. I'm always happy to connect with fellow researchers and builders.
